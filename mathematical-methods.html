Scientific decision fidelity科学决策保真

Under specified computational and decision settings, an object-specific decision is faithful when it matches the result of applying the stated rule to trusted reference scores.在相同的计算设置和决策规则下，如果系统对具体研究对象作出的决策与可信参考计算得到的结果一致，我们就称其具有科学决策保真性。

a=g(p⋆)a=g(p^\star)
  • a: the decision actually produced, such as the selected candidate list or the assignment of further testing.a：实际作出的决策，例如最终候选名单，或是否安排进一步检测。
  • p⋆: the vector of scores obtained by the trusted reference computation under the specified settings. The star denotes the reference computation.p⋆：在规定设置下，由可信参考计算得到的分数向量。星号表示参考计算。
  • g: the decision rule specified in advance, such as selecting the 402 highest scores or assigning testing when a score reaches a threshold.g：事先规定的决策规则，例如选择分数最高的 402 个候选，或分数达到阈值时安排检测。

Thus, g(p⋆) is the reference decision. The equality states the fidelity criterion: the actual decision must match that reference.将参考分数 p⋆ 代入规则 g，得到的就是参考决策。这个等式表示：系统实际作出的决策应与参考决策一致。

These settings specify the inputs, model, numerical precision, execution conditions, candidate set and tie handling. Decision fidelity concerns the output of the declared rule applied to reference scores. Evidence-path fidelity is assessed separately by comparing the agent’s reads and citations with those in a reference execution.比较前，需要确定输入、模型、数值精度、执行条件、候选集合和平局处理规则。这里的决策保真关注最终决策是否与参考分数按同一规则得到的结果一致；至于读了哪些文档、引用了哪些句子，则另行与参考运行记录比较。

01

Check outputs against their inputs用输入核验输出

For a linear layer, X has u rows and k inputs, W has v rows and k inputs, and b is a v-entry bias vector. The vector 1 contains ones; its subscript specifies its length.以线性层为例，输入 X 包含 u 行、k 个特征，权重 W 包含 v 行、k 个特征，b 是长度为 v 的偏置向量。公式中的 1 表示各项均为 1 的向量，下标表示向量长度。

Y=XW⊤+1ub⊤Y=XW^\top+\mathbf1_u b^\top

The expected row sums χrow and column sums χcol can be computed from X, W and b without forming the full reference product:根据输入 X、权重 W 和偏置 b，可以直接算出预期的行和 χrow 与列和 χcol，无需先重算整张输出矩阵：

χrow=X(W⊤1v)+1u(b⊤1v)\chi_{\mathrm{row}}=X(W^\top\mathbf1_v)+\mathbf1_u(b^\top\mathbf1_v)
χcol⊤=(1u⊤X)W⊤+ub⊤\chi_{\mathrm{col}}^\top=(\mathbf1_u^\top X)W^\top+u b^\top

Let Ŷ be the observed output. Compare its sums with χrow and χcol. For example, row i is flagged when the magnitude of its computed residual exceeds βᵣ,ᵢ:再将实际输出 Ŷ 的行和、列和与上述结果比较。例如，当第 i 行的校验残差超过允许的舍入误差界 βᵣ,ᵢ 时，就标记这一行：

∣r^res,i∣>βr,i|\widehat{r}_{\mathrm{res},i}|>\beta_{\mathrm{r},i}

The residual compares Ŷ1 with χrow. The bound β includes rounding in the declared layer computation and in the checksum calculation; column checks use the corresponding column bounds.这里的残差来自 Ŷ1 与 χrow 的差。误差界 β 同时考虑线性层计算和校验计算中的舍入误差；列检查采用同样的原则。

Why a checksum does not bound each score为什么校验和不能给出每个分数的误差界
E=(η−η−ηη),E1=0,1⊤E=0E=\begin{pmatrix}\eta&-\eta\\-\eta&\eta\end{pmatrix},\quad E\mathbf1=\mathbf0,\quad\mathbf1^\top E=\mathbf0

For any nonzero η, this error matrix has zero row and column sums. Cancellation can therefore hide individual errors; decision certification requires additional valid score bounds or trusted recomputation.只要 η 不为零，矩阵中就存在误差，但各行、各列求和后仍然为零。这说明正负误差可能相互抵消，掩盖具体数值的偏差。因此，要认证最终决策，还需要有效的逐分数误差界或可信重算。

Streaming verification with conservative fallback带保守回退的流式校验

Our streaming checker shares signed and absolute FP64 tile reductions across row and column checks. A conservative arithmetic guard resolves checks when the original verifier’s decision is unambiguous and falls back to that verifier otherwise. Across the 16 evaluated fault sources, it preserved flags, localization and bitwise recovery outputs while reducing total forward-and-verification time by 42.4% relative to our original FP64 implementation.流式校验在行、列检查之间共享带符号与绝对值的 FP64 分块归约。当保守误差界足以确定原始校验器的判定时，直接给出结果；否则回退到原始校验器。在评价的 16 个故障来源中，标记、定位及逐位恢复输出均与原实现一致，前向计算与验证的总耗时比原始 FP64 实现降低 42.4%。

Mathematical form of the guard判定逻辑的数学表述

For one row or column check, let r64 be the original FP64 residual and β64 its rounding-error bound. The signed margin z64 determines its flag f64: one when z64 > 0, zero otherwise. Let z̃ be the streaming estimate and κ a valid bound on its discrepancy from z64, including rounding in both the residual and bound calculations.对一次行或列检查，r64 是原始 FP64 校验残差，β64 是对应的舍入误差界。残差绝对值与误差界之差 z64 决定标记 f64：大于零时取 1，否则取 0。z̃ 是流式估计，κ 是它与 z64 之差的有效上界，包含残差与误差界计算中的舍入影响。

z64=|r64|−β64|z~−z64|≤κz_{64}=|r_{64}|-\beta_{64},\qquad |\widetilde z-z_{64}|\le\kappa
fstream={1z~−κ>00z~+κ<0f64otherwise=f64f_{\mathrm{stream}}=\begin{cases}1,&\widetilde z-\kappa>0\\0,&\widetilde z+\kappa<0\\f_{64},&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}=f_{64}

If the interval lies entirely above zero, flag the check; if it lies entirely below zero, pass it. An interval containing or touching zero triggers the original FP64 check. Under the stated bound, the streaming flag equals the original FP64 flag.区间完全高于零时标记，完全低于零时通过；包含或触及零时调用原始 FP64 检查。在上述误差界成立时，流式判定与原始校验器一致。

02

Locate and test the differences定位并检验差异

Pair a healthy reference state h with a candidate state c captured at the same computation site. For a coordinate or block set S, restoration corrects the candidate; transplantation introduces the captured differences into the reference.在同一个计算位置，分别保存健康参考状态 h 和候选状态 c。对坐标或块集合 S，恢复操作将对应数值换回参考值；移植操作则将候选值放入参考状态。

Here i indexes a tensor entry. When blocks are selected, S denotes the union of their entry coordinates. RS is the restored state and TS the transplanted state.i 表示张量条目的坐标；按块选择时，S 表示这些块包含的全部条目坐标。RS 是恢复后的状态，TS 是移植后的状态。

(RS)i={hi,i∈Sci,i∉S(R_S)_i=\begin{cases}h_i,&i\in S\\c_i,&i\notin S\end{cases}
(TS)i={ci,i∈Shi,i∉S(T_S)_i=\begin{cases}c_i,&i\in S\\h_i,&i\notin S\end{cases}

Continue with the same downstream computation F and compare endpoint D. Restoration tests recovery of D(F(h)); transplantation tests reproduction of D(F(c)). The two directions provide controls for one another. Empty and full edits, healthy repeats and fixed continuation conditions distinguish the intervention from reconstruction errors.继续相同的后续计算 F，比较终点 D。恢复实验检验是否恢复 D(F(h))，移植实验检验是否重现 D(F(c))；两个方向的干预互为对照。通过不替换数值、完整替换和健康重复等对照，并保持后续计算条件一致，可以区分干预效果与状态重建带来的误差。

03

Recompute selected objects选择性重算

For fixed threshold and Top-K rules, let d(q) be the L∞ distance to the decision boundary. A rule output can change only if the score displacement reaches that boundary.对于固定阈值或 Top-K 选择，分数变了并不意味着名单一定会变。只有分数位移达到决策边界，规则输出才可能改变。下面用 d(q) 表示分数向量到边界的 L∞ 距离。

q is a score vector and s the currently observed vector. Bg denotes the decision boundary, where g is not locally constant. The L∞ norm is the largest absolute coordinate difference, and dist∞ is distance measured with that norm.q 表示一个分数向量，s 是当前观察到的分数向量。Bg 表示规则 g 不再局部恒定的决策边界。L∞ 范数是各坐标绝对差的最大值，dist∞ 是按该范数计算的距离。

d(q)=dist⁡∞(q,Bg)d(q)=\operatorname{dist}_{\infty}(q,B_g)
g(s)≠g(p⋆) ⟹ ∥s−p⋆∥∞≥d(p⋆)g(s)\ne g(p^\star)\ \Longrightarrow\ \|s-p^\star\|_\infty\ge d(p^\star)

For Top-K membership with a strict cut-off gap, d(q) = (q(K) − q(K+1))/2. An unchanged score can enter or leave when competitors move. M uses current, per-object margins to allocate additional recomputation.对于按分数选取前 K 个对象的规则，如果入选线处没有平局，则 d(q) = (q(K) − q(K+1))/2。即使某个对象自身的分数没有变化，竞争对象的分数变化也可能使它进入或退出名单。M 就是根据各对象当前距离决策边界的远近，安排进一步重算。

For the vector of threshold decisions gi(q) = 1[qi ≥ τ], the boundary distance is d(q) = mini|qi − τ|.对全部对象的阈值决策 gi(q) = 1[qi ≥ τ]，边界距离为 d(q) = mini|qi − τ|。

K is the number selected; q(K) is the K-th largest score. In the threshold rule, τ is the specified threshold, i indexes an object, and 1[·] equals one when the condition holds and zero otherwise. R1 recomputes predictions identified by arithmetic checks; M adds objects whose current decision margin is below the empirical threshold ε, determined before evaluation.K 是入选数量，q(K) 是第 K 大的分数。在阈值规则中，τ 是规定阈值，i 是对象索引；1[·] 在条件成立时取 1，否则取 0。R1 重算由算术检查定位到的预测；M 追加重算当前决策余量小于经验阈值 ε 的对象，ε 在评价前确定。

From distance to an interval certificate从边界距离到区间认证

Suppose each score has a valid bound |si − p⋆i| ≤ ei. The admissible set lies within radius max ei of s. A radius smaller than d(s) cannot reach a boundary, so the rule is constant throughout the set.若每个分数具有有效误差界 |si − p⋆i| ≤ ei，可容许集合位于以 s 为中心、max ei 为半径的球内。半径小于 d(s) 时无法触及边界，规则在整个集合内为常值。

The non-negative ei bounds the error of score i. The admissible set is 𝒰 = {q : |qi − si| ≤ ei for every i}. Thus g(𝒰) is the set of decisions obtained from all score vectors allowed by these bounds.非负的 ei 是第 i 个分数的误差上界。可容许集合为 𝒰 = {q：对每个 i，都有 |qi − si| ≤ ei}。g(𝒰) 则是这些误差界允许的所有分数向量对应的决策集合。

max⁡iei<d(s) ⟹ g(U)={g(s)}\max_i e_i<d(s)\ \Longrightarrow\ g(\mathcal U)=\{g(s)\}

Full recomputation gives a singleton set; the specified tie rule then determines its decision, including at a tie.全量重算得到单点集合，规定的平局规则可以确定其决策，包括参考分数恰好平局的情况。

The displacement condition is necessary for a decision change; the interval condition is sufficient, not necessary, for certification.位移条件是决策变化的必要条件，区间条件是认证的充分而非必要条件。

A numerical example: K13418一个具体例子：K13418

In Case 2 (expanded-trigger), block 3, the healthy score was about 0.304710, below the testing threshold τ ≈ 0.30475175. After R1, the score was still about 0.305339, above the threshold, which added a testing assignment.以 Case 2（触发范围扩展型）的第 3 区组为例，K13418 的健康分数约为 0.304710，低于检测阈值 τ ≈ 0.30475175。R1 之后，它的分数仍约为 0.305339，高于阈值，因此比参考决策多安排了一项检测。

The positive residual, about 0.000629, crosses the healthy margin of about 0.000041. M uses only the current distance to the threshold, about 0.00059: it is smaller than ε = 0.009354, so this object is recomputed and the reference decision is recovered.约 0.000629 的向上偏移，已经越过了健康分数距阈值约 0.000041 的间隔。M 无需读取健康参考，只需检查当前分数距阈值约 0.00059，小于预定的 ε = 0.009354，就会将该对象纳入重算。重算后，检测分配恢复为参考决策。

K13418 is labeled a non-blocker in the benchmark; the changed decision adds an unnecessary test.K13418 在基准数据中标注为非阻断剂，这里多出的是一次不必要的检测。

Decision recovery versus full-state recovery决策恢复与完整状态恢复的成本

Recovery cost恢复成本

Recover the decision before the full state决策可以先于完整状态恢复

Let C(S) measure the cost of an admissible edit. The decision endpoint may be membership, order or testing assignment. The two recovery targets have different feasible sets:令 C(S) 表示允许编辑的成本。决策终点可以是名单成员、排序或检测分配。两种恢复成本具有不同的可行集合：

ℛ is the family of allowed coordinate sets S. F continues computation from the saved state; D extracts the endpoint being compared. ρD is the minimum cost of recovering that endpoint, and ρstate the minimum cost of reproducing the full reference state h. C(S) uses the same specified unit in both problems, such as replaced blocks or measured computation time; an infeasible minimum is +∞.ℛ 是允许选择的坐标集合 S 的全体。F 从保存状态继续计算，D 提取要比较的终点。ρD 是恢复该终点的最小成本，ρstate 是恢复完整参考状态 h 的最小成本。两者的 C(S) 使用同一规定单位，例如替换块数或实测计算时间；无可行方案时，最小值记为 +∞。

ρD=min⁡S∈RD(F(RS))=D(F(h))C(S)\rho_D=\min_{\substack{S\in\mathcal R\\D(F(R_S))=D(F(h))}}C(S)
ρstate=min⁡S∈RRS=hC(S)\rho_{\mathrm{state}}=\min_{\substack{S\in\mathcal R\\R_S=h}}C(S)
ρD≤ρstate\rho_D\le\rho_{\mathrm{state}}

With the same admissible edits, cost function and fixed deterministic continuation, full-state recovery is sufficient for endpoint recovery. This inclusion establishes the inequality.在相同允许编辑、成本函数和固定确定性后续计算下，完整状态恢复足以恢复终点，因此得到这个不等式。

Restore the execution state恢复执行状态

The agent’s decision is a = π(H). Here π is its decision procedure, and H includes the visible history, tool-derived variables and prepared decisions. Let H₀ be the saved state before reading a score table. Φ(H, p) denotes continuation from execution state H with score table p available to the tools.Agent 不只依赖眼前的文件，还会使用此前读到的信息和已经生成的变量、名单。我们用 H 表示这些执行状态，用 π 表示 Agent 据此作出决策的过程，即 a = π(H)。H₀ 是读取分数表前保存的状态。Φ(H, p) 表示从执行状态 H 继续分析，此时工具可读取的分数表为 p。

Hc=Φ(H0,pc),a=π(Hc)H_c=\Phi(H_0,p^c),\qquad a=\pi(H_c)

The vector pᶜ is the faulty score table; p⋆ is its trusted reference. File replacement makes the trusted table available, but the agent continues from Hc, the execution state formed using the faulty table:pᶜ 是故障分数表，p⋆ 是可信参考表。只替换文件后，工具可读取的表格已换成可信参考，但 Agent 仍从使用故障表后形成的执行状态 Hc 继续分析：

(pc,Hc) ⟶ (p⋆,Φ(Hc,p⋆))(p^c,H_c)\ \longrightarrow\ (p^\star,\Phi(H_c,p^\star))

Execution-state restoration returns to H₀ and continues with the reference table:恢复执行状态则先返回读取前的 H₀，再使用同一份参考表继续分析：

(pc,Hc) ⟶ (p⋆,Φ(H0,p⋆))(p^c,H_c)\ \longrightarrow\ (p^\star,\Phi(H_0,p^\star))

05

Check the final decision核对最终决策

𝒰 contains the score vectors permitted by valid numerical evidence. acert is the single decision shared by all of them. a is the actual final decision.𝒰 包含有效数值证据允许的分数向量，acert 是这些向量共同给出的唯一决策。a 是实际最终决策。

p⋆∈U,g(U)={acert},a=acert⟹a=g(p⋆)\begin{gathered}p^\star\in\mathcal U,\quad g(\mathcal U)=\{a_{\mathrm{cert}}\},\quad a=a_{\mathrm{cert}}\\ \Longrightarrow\quad a=g(p^\star)\end{gathered}

Certification requires three checks: the score vectors permitted by valid evidence include the trusted reference; the rule gives the same decision throughout that set; and the submitted decision matches that result. If the agent has already used incorrect values, their effects on its history, variables and previously prepared decisions must also be addressed. Before recording the final decision, the system checks the certified result and the current input data again.认证需要确认三件事：有效证据允许的分数范围包含可信参考；规则对这个范围内的分数都给出同一决策；系统最终提交的决策与它一致。如果 Agent 已经使用过错误数值，还需要处理历史记录、变量和此前决策中保留的影响。记录最终决策前，系统会再次核对认证结果和当前输入数据。

In the hERG single-request experiment, every certificate came from full trusted recomputation.在 hERG 单请求实验中，每份证书均来自全量可信重算。
